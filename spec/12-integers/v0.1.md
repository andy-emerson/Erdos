# Growing Unsigned Integers: Specification v0.1

Oct 30, 2026 · Records the author's decisions of Oct 30, 2026, as finally corrected. Details that follow from them are marked **(coordinator's call)**. Unsigned magnitudes only; sign and complex directions are out of scope. Checked against [`../../RULES.md`](../../RULES.md) by the coordinator. Nothing is built or run.

## 1. Builds

Five builds. Each is one idea, measured against Control, with no combinations. All are in Nim 2.2.14 (the 11-matrix pin and flags), self-contained.

1. **Control** (record). Nim's `uint64` with its own operators and `std/math.gcd`. Nothing else is built. Overflow wraps; it is reported, not solved.
2. **Power-of-2 flag** (special cases). Control's `uint64` with bit 63 meaning "is a power of 2", so values have 63 bits. Every result recomputes the flag as $r \ne 0 \wedge r \mathbin{\&} (r-1) = 0$. The flag is used:
   - in multiply, as a shift;
   - in exact division, as a shift when the divisor is flagged, or as $1 \ll (\text{ctz}\,a - \text{ctz}\,b)$ when the dividend is;
   - in gcd, as $1 \ll \min(\text{ctz}\,a, \text{ctz}\,b)$.
3. **Even flag** (special case). The same, with bit 63 meaning "is even", recomputed as $r \mathbin{\&} 1 = 0$. It is used only in gcd, to skip the shared-power step when an operand is odd. It duplicates the low bit; it is tested at the author's request.
4. **Growing** (general improvement). Schoolbook words, lowest first, with no leading zero words. Each value is one `malloc`'d block (header word + words) and immutable. The routines are schoolbook add and subtract; compare; shifts; multiply on a 64×64→128 product (gcc's `unsigned __int128`); Hensel exact division; and binary gcd over many words. There are no special cases. The word size $W$ is a parameter: 64 now, and it must also work at 32.
5. **Byte encoding** (general improvement, memory). A value is its byte length $L$ as an unsigned LEB128 varint, then $L$ magnitude bytes, little-endian. This is the standard length-delimited form, as in Protocol Buffers' byte strings and CBOR's bignums. **(Coordinator's call:** chosen over plain LEB128, which takes an eighth more bytes and outgrows Growing from 8 words on, and whose 7-bit regrouping would dominate conversion.**)** Every operation decodes both operands to words, runs Growing's routine, and encodes the result: **2 decodes and 1 encode**.

## 2. Operations

Six operations run on **every build, with no exceptions**: add, subtract (the build's normal subtraction), absolute difference $\lvert a - b \rvert$ (compare, then subtract the smaller from the larger), multiply, exact division and gcd.

A result that is not exact (wraparound, overflow past 64 or 63 bits, or subtract with $a < b$) is **counted outside the clock** against the exact value and reported. It is never prevented.

## 3. Operands

- **Jobs:** five size classes, $k \in \{1, 2, 4, 8, 16\}$ words, with 1024 operations per operation and job **(coordinator's call)**. Every build gets the same operands, from one seeded Python generator.
- **Bit lengths:** uniform within the class. In job 1 they run from 1 to 63 bits, so the flag builds can hold the operands.
- **Exact division:** $a = bq$, with $b$ and $q$ at half size.
- **gcd:** $a = gx$ and $b = gy$, with $g$, $x$ and $y$ at half size, so the gcd is not trivial.
- **Subtract and absolute difference:** the same pairs, in random order.
- **Rates:**
  - even values occur at 1/2, the natural rate of a uniform draw;
  - each drawn quantity is a power of 2 with probability 1/16. **(Coordinator's call:** a placeholder, so that the flag's paths run. A uniform draw almost never gives a power of 2. The author should confirm or set the rate.**)**

## 4. Measures

For **every build × operation × job**:
- **time per operation;**
- **bytes per value** (accounted, heap figure beside);
- **peak bytes during the operation** (accounted).

For the word builds both space measures are 8 bytes, with no allocation. That is trivial, and reported as such, subtraction included.

**Ratios and pairings:**
- **job 1:** each build × Control;
- **jobs 2–16:** Byte encoding × Growing;
- **within each build:** absolute difference × subtract.

**Timing and verdicts:** rule-m3 ([`../11-matrix/research/rule-m3.md`](../11-matrix/research/rule-m3.md)), items 4–9: the rounds, calibration, reps, pinning, CPU time, idle and disturbance checks, a null row, $h = \max(5\%, \text{null spread})$, and its verdict thresholds. A difference within the noise gets no verdict.

**Tiny operations (coordinator's call):**
- the operands are precomputed in arrays that fit L1;
- every result is stored and checksummed after the clock, so none can be removed;
- the job-1 binary is scalar (`-fno-tree-vectorize`);
- callgrind instruction counts are reported beside the times.
