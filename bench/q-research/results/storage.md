## Known asymmetries

- byte's multiply with a partial top limb is mpn_mul_n on the full limbs plus two mpn_addmul_1 rows, against one mpn_mul_n for limb and pow2: an algorithmic cost of packing, not a traffic cost; mul is reported apart and 'all but mul' leaves it out. One-limb operands use the same inline 64x64 product in every variant.
- byte uses unaligned limb pointers into GMP (fine on x86-64 with this GMP build; see the GMP path above) and writes the partial top limb by read-modify-write of 8 bytes, which is only safe single-threaded.
- re-stores always malloc a new vector, copy, zero-fill and free (no realloc or growth in place).
